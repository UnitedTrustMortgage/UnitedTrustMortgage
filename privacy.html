Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect information submitted through the United Trust Mortgage website.

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains our views and practices concerning privacy, and how they may pertain to you as a user of our system.

"You" or "Your" means you, a participant in or user of the myunitedtrust.com website. "We," "Our," or "Us" means UTM Financial, LLC, the operator of United Trust Mortgage. "Our Website" also means myunitedtrust.com. United Trust Mortgage is a DBA of UTM Financial, LLC (NMLS #2591548).

All information transmitted, printed, or otherwise submitted to UTM Financial through this Website remains your property; however, UTM Financial will be free to use that information for any lawful purpose as detailed below. This Website contains links to other websites. UTM Financial is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of such sites.

Collected Information

UTM Financial automatically collects and/or tracks the following information:

  • Information knowingly provided by you through online forms including your name, street address, e-mail address, telephone number, date of birth, income information, employment information, and other personal, financial or demographic information (collectively, "Sensitive Information");
  • Web page HTTP headers (server domain names, IP address, client computer type, browser and operating system);
  • Information concerning pages visitors access and referring URLs; and
  • E-mail addresses of visitors who communicate with UTM Financial via e-mail.

Submitting information is voluntary. However, to receive information on a loan-related product from a Loan Provider, you must submit the required information.

Use of Data Collected

We use collected information to enhance your experience, offer relevant content, improve our Website, conduct statistical analysis, and for marketing and promotional purposes. Information may be added to our databases and used for future communications regarding products and services.

Disclosure of Data to Third Parties

By using this Website, you agree that you may be contacted even if your number is on a do-not-call registry. If you choose to provide personal information, it may be shared with trusted third parties and financial service providers for the purpose of funding your loan request.

By submitting an application for credit, you authorize us to use your information to obtain additional information from third parties as needed to evaluate your application. Third parties include consumer reporting agencies, credit bureaus, and other outside parties as permitted by applicable laws.

Your Credit

All lenders we work with can offer free rate quotes with no impact to your credit. Some Loan Providers issue pre-approvals without any credit check; others use a soft credit check (no impact to FICO score). Once pre-approved, some lenders may perform a hard credit inquiry to finalize financing, and will ask your permission before doing so.

Information Sharing

UTM Financial reserves the right to release user information: (1) when the Website is believed to be used in violation of Terms and Conditions or to commit unlawful acts; (2) as required by law or legal process; or (3) in connection with a business transfer.

Information Collection Processes (Cookies)

UTM Financial may employ "cookies" to customize your access to the Website. Most browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can modify your browser to decline them. You may still use our Website if you decline cookies, with some limitations.

Electronic Disclosure

By submitting your information via this Website, you agree to receive notifications, disclosures, and documents electronically. You may withdraw consent by exiting the application website prior to submission, but cannot withdraw consent after submission.

Your Opt-Out Rights

You may opt-out of receiving communications from us by not submitting your information. To opt-out of e-mail communications, send your request to: info@myunitedtrust.com

TCPA Disclosure

By providing your telephone number on this Website, you consent pursuant to the TCPA to receive calls and text messages (including those using an automatic telephone dialing system or prerecorded voice) from UTM Financial, United Trust Mortgage, and our affiliates and partners at the number provided, even if listed on any Do-Not-Call registry. Consent is not a condition of obtaining services. Standard message and data rates may apply. You may opt out at any time.

Special Notice to California Residents

We will not share personal information about you to the extent prohibited by applicable California law. California residents may request information about our disclosures of personal information to third parties for direct marketing purposes once per calendar year by contacting: info@myunitedtrust.com

Our Commitment to Children's Privacy

We never knowingly collect or maintain personal information from persons under the age of 18. If such information is inadvertently collected, it will be immediately deleted from our database.

Security

Access to your information is strictly limited to employees who need it to perform specific job functions.
