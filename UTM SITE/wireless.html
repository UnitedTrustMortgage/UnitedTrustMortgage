Wireless & SMS Policy

How United Trust Mortgage uses text messaging — including TCPA consent, opt-out instructions, and message frequency.

Last updated:
By providing your cell phone number, you have provided United Trust Mortgage (NMLS #2591548), its third-party service providers and/or finance providers with consent to send you text messages in conjunction with the services you have requested. Your cellular provider's Msg & Data Rates may apply.

You understand the text messages we send may be seen by anyone with access to your phone. Accordingly, you should take steps to safeguard your phone and your text messages if you want them to remain private.

Please notify us immediately if you change mobile numbers or plan to provide your phone to another person.

If we modify this SMS Wireless Policy, we will notify you by sending you a text message with a link to the new policy. We may terminate our text message program at any time.

Consent to Contact

You agree and consent to be contacted by United Trust Mortgage, our agents, employees, attorneys, affiliates, subsequent creditors, loan servicing companies, third-party collectors, third-party service providers and finance providers through the use of email, and/or telephone calls and/or SMS text messages to your cellular, home or work phone numbers, as well as any other phone number you have provided, including the use of automatic telephone dialing systems, autodialers, or an artificial or prerecorded voice.

TCPA Consent

By providing your telephone number and submitting your information, you are providing your prior express written consent pursuant to the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) to receive telephone calls (including calls using an automatic telephone dialing system or an artificial or prerecorded voice) and SMS/MMS text messages from United Trust Mortgage, our affiliates, agents, service providers, and third-party partners at the telephone number you provide, even if that number is listed on any Federal, State, or internal Do-Not-Call list. You understand that your consent is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services from us. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You may revoke your consent at any time.

Opt-Out / STOP

If you wish to stop receiving marketing text messages from United Trust Mortgage, reply to any text message we have sent you and type STOP. If you wish to stop receiving all text messages, type STOP ALL. Your stop request will become effective within one business day. You may also stop text messages by calling or emailing us at the contact information below.

Help or Support

If you need our contact information or information on how to stop text messages, reply to any text message we have sent you and type HELP. We will send you a text message with this information. It is our policy to send no more than six messages per month.
