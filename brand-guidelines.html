01 — Brand Essence

Capital that closes. Without compromise.

United Trust Mortgage is a nationwide Non-QM and specialty mortgage brokerage for borrowers and scenarios traditional banks decline — DSCR, bank statement, P&L, asset depletion, conventional, and jumbo. 02 — Voice & Tone

How we sound

Confident and precise. Short, declarative sentences. We state outcomes, not adjectives. Plain English over jargon; when a term is technical (DSCR, LTV), we use it correctly and briefly. Never hypey, never pushy — the calm authority of a firm that closes hard deals routinely.

Write like this

  • "Capital that closes. Without compromise."
  • "Underwritten with judgment, closed with speed."
  • "Qualify on rental income, not your W-2."
  • "No impact to your credit score."

Not like this

  • "Unlock your dream with our AMAZING rates!!!"
  • Stacked exclamation points or all-caps hype
  • Vague promises ("best rates guaranteed")
  • Jargon walls with no plain-English anchor
03 — Logo

Logo & wordmark

The primary mark is the UTM house monogram with the United Trust Mortgage wordmark. In tight UI (e.g. the nav) the wordmark may shorten to United Trust. Always pair the mark with the full legal name in footers and legal contexts.

Usage rules

Do

  • Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the monogram
  • Minimum digital width ~120px so the wordmark stays legible
  • Place on bone, paper, or ink backgrounds with strong contrast
  • Use the full "United Trust Mortgage" name in footers / legal

Don't

  • Stretch, condense, rotate, or add shadows/glows
  • Recolor the mark outside the brand palette
  • Place on busy photos or low-contrast fields
  • Crowd it against text or other logos

10 — Imagery & Icons

Imagery & iconography

Photography: architectural, real-estate, and interior imagery with natural light and calm tones that sit comfortably beside the bone palette. Avoid stocky "handshake/headset" clichés. Icons: thin line icons (≈2–2.5px stroke, rounded caps) in ink or emerald — never filled or multicolor. Keep figures and data in the ledger style (Fraunces + champagne).

11 — At a glance

Do & Don't

Do

  • Lead with bone + ink; use emerald as the one accent
  • Let whitespace and hairlines carry structure
  • Set headlines in Fraunces, labels in mono
  • Reserve champagne for figures and crop details
  • Keep CTAs to one ink primary per view

Don't

  • Reintroduce navy/gold (the old DSCR palette)
  • Use rounded corners over 4px or drop-shadows for "pop"
  • Set body copy in champagne or in the serif
  • Stack multiple loud CTAs or gradients
  • Use emoji as primary iconography in chrome
